How to survive

  • WASD / arrows: move
  • Left click (hold): gather, chop, mine, drink, open doors, feed fires
  • Right click or Shift+click: take apart something you built (full refund)
  • C: crafting · B: build mode (1–4 pick, click to place)
  • F: eat · E: drink from nearby water · Z: sleep (in a shelter at night)
  • P or Esc: pause · Esc also closes panels and leaves build mode

Tips

  • Pick up sticks and pebbles by hand, then craft a Stone Axe to chop trees for wood.
  • Nights are cold. You lose health outside unless you're near a lit campfire or inside a shelter.
  • A shelter is a fully floored room closed in by walls and a closed door.
  • Campfires burn wood. Click one while holding wood to add fuel. Cook a Berry Pie next to one.
  • When hunger and thirst are both above half, your health slowly comes back.
