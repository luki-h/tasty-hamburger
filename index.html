How to survive

  • WASD: move · Space: jump / swim up · Shift: sprint · Mouse: look around
  • Left click (hold): break blocks, pick berries
  • Right click: place the selected block, eat, open/close doors, add fuel to campfires
  • 1–9 or mouse wheel: choose a hotbar slot
  • C: crafting · F: eat · E: drink (look at water or stand in it) · Z: sleep
  • P or Esc: pause

Tips

  • Break leaves with your hands for sticks, and grey speckled gravel for pebbles.
  • Craft a Stone Axe to chop logs, and a Stone Pickaxe to mine stone.
  • Turn logs into planks, then build walls, a roof and a door around you.
  • Nights are cold. You lose health outside unless you're near a lit campfire or inside a shelter.
  • A shelter has a roof over your head and walls on all four sides. A door only counts when it's closed.
  • Campfires burn out. Right-click one while holding sticks or logs to add fuel. Cook a Berry Pie next to one.
  • When hunger and thirst are both above half, your health slowly comes back.
